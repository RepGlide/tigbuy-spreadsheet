A visible number is incomplete until you can state what it is, how it is measured, what it covers, and when it held.

Percent Without a Base Is Incomplete

A Percentage Is a Fraction, Not a Fact

A percentage is only meaningful with a base, a scope, a time, and a source.

  • Denominator.

    What the percentage is a share of. Without the base, the share cannot be read.

  • Scope and range.

    What the percentage applies to, and over what set of items or cases.

  • Calculation time.

    When the percentage was computed. A figure changes when its base changes.

  • Source.

    Where the percentage comes from, so it can be checked at its origin.

Leave the Number Unresolved

When a Context Is Missing

A gap is not a prompt to guess. It is a signal to stop.

  • 1

    Mark it “Not stated.” Leave the missing context visible instead of guessing.

  • 2

    Do not compare or rank. A number without its basis cannot be sorted against another.

  • 3

    Do not fill the gap. A guess in the empty slot is worse than an empty slot.

  • 4

    Re-read at the source. Confirm the missing context where the number lives.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a TigBuy spreadsheet?

A TigBuy spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links for use with agent shopping services. It is a pointer to browse and re-check, not an official, live, or complete product catalog.

Is this website the official TigBuy service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by TigBuy or Google.

What are the four number contexts?

Label says what the number represents, Unit says how it is measured, Basis says which items, quantities, or conditions it includes, and Time says when it held. A number missing any of these is incomplete.

Why does a percentage need a base?

A percentage is only meaningful with a denominator, a scope or range it applies to, the time it was calculated, and its source. Without a base, a percentage is incomplete and cannot be ranked.

When can two numbers be compared?

Only when their currency, quantity, options, time point, and included items all match. Numbers that differ in any of these cannot be directly sorted or ranked.

What should I do when a number is missing a context?

Leave it unresolved and mark the missing context as not stated. Do not fill in the gap with a guess, and do not compare or rank the number until its contexts are known.
